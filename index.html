I am an MSc student in Geoinformatics Engineering at Politecnico di Milano and a Research Assistant at Lund University with Dr. Yuan Liao. My work sits at the intersection of causal inference, urban mobility, and geospatial data, with a focus on how neighborhoods adapt under macroeconomic shocks and how the built environment shapes socioeconomic differences in mobility.

Recent work includes a hierarchical panel design around energy-price shocks using large-scale POI-visit data across China and the US, and a multilevel study in Sweden linking satellite foundation-model embeddings of the built environment to SES–mobility gradients. I previously worked as a Solutions Engineer on provincial highway data systems in Hunan.
